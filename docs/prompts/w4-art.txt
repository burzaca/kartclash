You cannot open a browser, so the PM will judge the look. Compensate by following the proven look
recipe EXACTLY — this is the difference between a polished game and a toy-block game:
  - NEVER set flatShading true on any material. Use high segment counts: SphereGeometry 24-32 segments,
    LatheGeometry 20-24 profile points, TorusGeometry 24x12 or better.
  - Build the body, chassis and the driver from LATHE profiles (a stack of radius/height points) and
    rounded boxes — not from stacked cubes or capsules. A kart chassis that tapers, a helmet that
    curves, a driver torso that rounds off.
  - Details do more than polygons: helmet visor stripe, number plate on the nose, a spoiler, exhaust
    pipes, seat belt straps, wheel rims with a hub, steering wheel. Give the driver visible arms on the
    wheel and legs on the footwell.
  - Colours come from the spec object passed in (color, trim, skin, helmet) so the six racers look
    like six different characters. Use MeshStandardMaterial with sensible roughness/metalness; no
    textures, no images, no external files of any kind.
  - Author every model facing LOCAL +Z (nose at +Z, face at +Z, left side at -X) because the
    orientation formula in section 4 is fixed: group.rotation.y = Math.PI/2 - kart.heading.
  - Set castShadow / receiveShadow on the kart parts, and keep the whole kart within roughly a 2.2 m
    radius and 1.6 m tall so the chase camera framing works.

updateKartModel(group, kart, dt) is called every animation frame for every racer: it must apply the
visual state the physics writes (see section 0: kart.lean, wheelSpin, steerVis, boost.time,
drift.tier, star, invisible, shrunk, spin, air.y, hitFlash) and animate the wheels, the flame, the
shield, the wobble, the scale and the transparency. Do not allocate new objects inside it — do all
allocation once at build time and only mutate transforms/materials per frame.

createFX is a single pooled particle system: one InstancedMesh of capacity at least 1500, a free list,
no allocation in update(dt), instances hidden by scaling to zero. It has to survive several racers
drifting and boosting at the same time at 60 fps. Colour per instance via instanceColor. Additive
blending for boost flame and sparks, opaque for dust and confetti.
